YouTube aur TikTok setup

Dua: pehle YouTube ka 01–04 setup karo, phir TikTok ka 05–07. Har English box ko copy karke usi jagah paste karo.

Algorithm ki asal baat: koi secret switch reach nahi deta. YouTube viewer ka click, watch aur satisfaction dekhta hai. TikTok watch behavior aur interactions ke saath caption, sound aur hashtags se topic samajhta hai. Is page ka maqsad channel ko ek saaf niche aur consistent identity dena hai.

01

YouTube channel ki pehchan

  1. YouTube Studio kholo.
  2. Customization → Profile kholo.
  3. Neeche ka channel name aur handle lagao. Handle unavailable ho to sirf diya fallback try karo.

03

YouTube channel settings

  1. Studio → Settings → Channel → Basic info.
  2. Country of residence mein apna asal country select karo. Monetization eligibility isi setting se linked hai.
  3. Channel keywords paste karo.
  4. Advanced settings → Audience: No, set this channel as not made for kids.
  5. Feature eligibility: phone/identity verification jo available ho complete karo.

04

YouTube upload defaults

Studio → Settings → Upload defaults. Yeh defaults sirf browser uploads par automatically lagte hain.

  • Visibility: Private. SEO page se sab paste aur check karke Schedule/Public karna.
  • Title: khali chhoro; har video ka final title SEO pack mein hai.
  • Description: khali chhoro; har video ka exact description SEO pack mein hai.
  • Category: Education.
  • Language: English.
  • License: Standard YouTube License.
  • Comments: On; potentially inappropriate comments review ke liye hold.
  • Captions: English captions upload/check karna.
  • Altered content: realistic synthetic scene ho to upload par honestly disclose karna.
  • Third-party AI training: off rakho jab tak Saif khud permission na den.

Home tab par pehle Uploads, phir Shorts, phir playlist lagao. Teen videos publish hone ke baad best long ko returning viewers ke liye feature karo.

05

TikTok account setup

  1. TikTok mein naya account banao aur email/phone verify karo.
  2. Personal Account hi rakho; Public account on karo. Creator ke liye isi mein analytics aur general sound library milti hai.
  3. Nickname, username aur bio neeche se paste karo.

06

TikTok privacy aur upload settings

  • Account: Public Personal Account.
  • Language: English content; app language jo asaan ho rakh sakti ho.
  • Comments: Everyone; filters on.
  • Duet aur Stitch: On, jab video mein sensitive personal information na ho.
  • Downloads: Off, taake clean copies doosre accounts par asaani se repost na hon.
  • Video: original 1080 × 1920, 9:16, TikTok watermark ke baghair upload.
  • Captions: English auto captions ko post se pehle correct karo.
  • Cover: SEO pack ka cover text center safe area mein.
  • Disclosure: AI-generated realistic visuals ka label option aaye to honestly select karo.
07

Har upload ke baad algorithm check

  1. SEO pack ka exact title/caption use karo; random #fyp aur unrelated trend mat lagao.
  2. Jo main search phrase caption mein hai, wahi video ki voice aur captions mein naturally hona chahiye.
  3. YouTube par pehle 24 ghantay Home/Suggested CTR aur audience retention dekho.
  4. TikTok par average watch time, full-video watches, search traffic, shares aur saves dekho.
  5. Weak video foran delete/re-upload mat karo. Data note karo aur aglay video ka opening/cover improve karo.
  6. Kisi ek video ka fail hona poore channel ko algorithm penalty nahi deta.
