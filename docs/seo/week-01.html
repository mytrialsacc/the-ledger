Description
A trusted church treasurer. Nine years of deposits. One account that had already been closed.

This composite financial fraud documentary follows Dolores after a bank tells her that the account she has been funding every Friday was closed nine months earlier. The receipts in her blue envelope show forty one thousand, eight hundred and sixteen dollars. The bank record shows nothing.

The story traces how a trusted church custodian used separate ledgers, changed account numbers, and moved new deposits to cover old withdrawals. It is a fictional composite built from recurring patterns in insider and community financial fraud.
