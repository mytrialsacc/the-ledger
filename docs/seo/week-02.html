Romance Scam: He Never Asked Her For Money

Final title
Romance Scam: He Never Asked Her For Money
Description
He never asked her for money. Not once. That was the whole design.

This romance scam documentary follows Evelyn, a seventy-nine-year-old widow who meets Arthur through an online history forum. Over three years, she sends forty-seven thousand, three hundred and twelve dollars to nine different accounts while every request arrives as a temporary delay, document, or release charge.

The story examines how an online romance scam can grow through emotional trust, old photographs, changing account names, and small payments that never feel like one large emergency. It is a fictional composite built from recurring patterns in elder financial abuse and relationship fraud.
