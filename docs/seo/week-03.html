Roofing Scam: The Roof Was Never Fixed
Description
The county has a file four inches thick. It begins with a roof that was never fixed.

This roofing scam documentary follows Leon Mercer, who collected deposits from nineteen houses. Seven roofs were opened, none were finished, and homeowners paid a total of eighty-four thousand, two hundred and nineteen dollars for work that did not exist.

The story examines how a contractor fraud scheme uses storm damage, real permits, borrowed license numbers, five business names, partial repairs, and fifty-percent deposits to keep new jobs funding old ones. It is a fictional composite built from recurring patterns in home-repair and financial fraud.
