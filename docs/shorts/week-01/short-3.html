Harold was the church treasurer. He gave Dolores a receipt from the office book and told her the money would sit in the church reserve.

For years, Dolores trusted the paper more than her memory. She kept each receipt in a blue envelope in the kitchen drawer. When a new green deposit slip appeared, she noticed that its account number was longer than the old one.

Dolores mentioned it to Harold in the corridor. He looked at the slip and said the bank had changed its numbering system.

Nine years later, the teller asked for the original slips. Dolores placed them on the counter until the envelope was empty.
