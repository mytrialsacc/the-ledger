The first withdrawal request arrived in April of the sixth year. Frank needed twelve thousand dollars for a medical procedure. Calvin moved Frank's name to the top of the list and searched for a deposit large enough to cover him.

There was no large deposit that week. Calvin sold his sedan and sent Frank the money. He said the transfer came from a matured position. Frank thanked him and told two neighbours that Calvin had handled the request quickly.

In May, five more people asked for withdrawals. Calvin gave each person a new date. He stopped sending statements in June and said the printer had failed.

Nora drove to the office near the courthouse. The glass doors were locked. A paper notice belonged to the landlord.

The spreadsheet held three hundred and twelve names. One hundred and eighty six people had received some returns. The rest had paper balances.
